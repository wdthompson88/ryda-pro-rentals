# RYDA Sample Operating Agreement

Document status: redacted sample draft for review only.
Version: sample v0.1
Last updated: 2026-05-19

This sample is provided to show the structure RYDA expects each single-purpose
vehicle LLC agreement to follow. It is not legal advice, not a binding
agreement, and not approved for signature until reviewed by counsel.

## 1. Parties

This Operating Agreement is entered into by the members of `[RYDA ASSET LLC]`,
a Florida limited liability company formed to own and operate one specific
vehicle or vessel.

Each member is admitted only after identity verification, payment clearance,
and execution of the required subscription and management documents.

## 2. Purpose

The LLC exists solely to:

- acquire, hold, insure, operate, maintain, and ultimately sell the asset;
- provide member use rights consistent with the booking rules;
- hold operating reserves and insurance proceeds; and
- engage RYDA as service provider under a separate Management Services
  Agreement.

The LLC may not operate an unrelated business, pool assets across vehicles, or
issue interests for investment purposes.

## 3. Membership Interests

The LLC is divided into a fixed number of membership shares listed in Exhibit A.
Each member's capital contribution, share count, and percentage interest are
recorded in the member register.

No member receives a public security, token, or interest in RYDA LLC. The
member owns an LLC membership interest tied to the specific asset.

## 4. Governance

The LLC is member-managed. Routine operations are delegated to RYDA under the
Management Services Agreement, but material LLC decisions remain with members.

Unless a stricter threshold is required by law, a 75 percent supermajority of
membership interests is required for:

- sale or replacement of the asset;
- material modification of the asset;
- additional capital calls;
- admission of a transferee member;
- amendment of this agreement;
- termination or replacement of RYDA as service provider; and
- early dissolution of the LLC.

## 5. Member Use Rights

Use rights are allocated according to share count and the Booking Rules &
Fair-Use Policy. Peak windows, cancellation rights, damage obligations, guest
rules, and extended-trip approvals are administered consistently across all
members.

Members must comply with insurance conditions, age requirements, operator
qualification rules, and market-specific handover procedures.

## 6. Reserves and Expenses

The LLC maintains reserves for insurance deductibles, routine maintenance,
registration, storage, detailing, and expected operating expenses. Members are
responsible for their pro-rata share of approved LLC costs.

RYDA may recommend reserve levels, but material capital calls require member
approval as described above.

## 7. Transfers

Transfers are subject to a minimum hold period, transferee eligibility review,
right-of-first-refusal mechanics, and execution of transfer documentation.

No transfer is effective until the LLC member register is updated and the
transferee agrees to this Operating Agreement.

## 8. Defaults

A member is in default if they fail to pay required amounts, violate usage
rules, cause insurance coverage to be voided, or materially breach this
agreement.

Remedies may include suspension of booking rights, reimbursement obligations,
forced transfer procedures, and recovery of enforcement costs, subject to
counsel-approved final terms.

## 9. Sale and Dissolution

At the planned exit date, or earlier by supermajority vote, RYDA will coordinate
valuation support and present sale options to the members. Net proceeds are
applied first to LLC liabilities and reserves, then distributed according to
membership interests.
