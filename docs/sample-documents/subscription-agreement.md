# RYDA Sample Subscription Agreement

Document status: redacted sample draft for review only.
Version: sample v0.1
Last updated: 2026-05-19

This sample describes the member admission document used when a buyer joins a
single-purpose RYDA asset LLC. It is not legal advice and is not approved for
signature until reviewed by counsel.

## 1. Subscription

The subscriber applies to purchase `[NUMBER]` membership share(s) in
`[RYDA ASSET LLC]` for the capital contribution shown on the final order
summary.

Admission is conditioned on payment clearance, identity verification, execution
of the Operating Agreement, and acceptance by the LLC.

## 2. Subscriber Information

The final agreement records:

- legal name;
- email and notice address;
- date of birth confirmation;
- verified identity status;
- share count;
- capital contribution;
- asset and LLC name; and
- funding method.

Sensitive identity documents are not attached to this sample.

## 3. Acknowledgments

The subscriber acknowledges that:

- the membership interest is tied to a specific LLC and asset;
- the interest is not a share of RYDA LLC;
- the asset is acquired for member use and not passive investment return;
- resale may be restricted by transfer rules and minimum hold periods;
- operating costs, depreciation, damage, and insurance risk can affect value;
  and
- the subscriber has had the opportunity to consult legal and tax advisors.

## 4. Representations

The subscriber represents that they:

- are at least 28 years old unless a stricter rule applies;
- are not prohibited from transacting with RYDA or the LLC;
- will provide accurate KYC and driver-eligibility information;
- will comply with the Operating Agreement and Booking Rules; and
- are purchasing for personal access and use, not guaranteed profit.

## 5. Closing

Upon acceptance, payment clearance, and required signatures, RYDA records the
subscriber in the LLC member register and provides access to the member portal.

## 6. Cancellation and Refunds

Refund, cancellation, and failed-verification treatment are governed by the
final purchase terms, payment processor rules, and the Operating Agreement.
