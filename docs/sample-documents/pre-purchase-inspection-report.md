# RYDA Sample Pre-Purchase Inspection Report

Document status: redacted sample.
Version: sample v0.1
Last updated: 2026-05-19

This sample shows the inspection format RYDA expects before an LLC acquires a
vehicle. The actual report is prepared by an independent marque specialist or
qualified inspection provider.

## 1. Asset Snapshot

- Asset: `[YEAR MAKE MODEL]`
- VIN: `[REDACTED]`
- Odometer: `[MILES]`
- Seller: `[REDACTED]`
- Inspection location: `[CITY, STATE]`
- Inspector: `[SHOP / TECHNICIAN]`
- Inspection date: `[DATE]`

## 2. Document Review

- Title status checked
- Lien search reviewed
- Accident-history report reviewed
- Service history reviewed
- Open recalls checked
- Warranty or certified-pre-owned status reviewed

## 3. Mechanical Inspection

The inspection covers engine, transmission, suspension, brakes, steering,
cooling, electrical systems, diagnostics, fluid condition, tires, wheels, and
visible underbody condition.

## 4. Cosmetic Inspection

The inspection records paint condition, panel gaps, glass, interior wear,
convertible roof or removable-panel operation, trim, lighting, and prior repair
indicators.

## 5. Road Test

The inspector records startup behavior, idle quality, transmission behavior,
braking, alignment, suspension noise, steering feel, warning lights, and any
abnormal drivability observations.

## 6. Findings

Sample findings:

- No active diagnostic trouble codes.
- Brake-pad life within acceptable range.
- Tires require replacement within `[X]` months.
- Minor cosmetic wear on lower front splitter.
- Service records show annual maintenance through `[DATE]`.

## 7. Acquisition Recommendation

Status: conditional pass.

Required before closing:

- confirm clean title;
- confirm lien payoff instructions;
- price tire replacement into reserve;
- attach photo log to LLC record; and
- confirm insurance binding before pickup.
