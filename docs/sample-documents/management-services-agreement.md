# RYDA Sample Management Services Agreement

Document status: redacted sample draft for review only.
Version: sample v0.1
Last updated: 2026-05-19

This sample describes the service relationship between a single-purpose asset
LLC and RYDA. It is not legal advice and is not approved for signature until
reviewed by counsel.

## 1. Parties

This Management Services Agreement is between `[RYDA ASSET LLC]` as asset owner
and RYDA LLC as service provider.

RYDA does not own the LLC's asset and does not control member votes. RYDA is
hired to perform operating services for the LLC.

## 2. Scope of Services

RYDA coordinates:

- acquisition diligence and closing logistics;
- insurance procurement and renewal support;
- storage, parking, marina, or hanger coordination;
- routine service, detailing, and preventive maintenance;
- booking calendar administration;
- member handover, return, and condition documentation;
- vendor coordination and invoice tracking;
- quarterly condition reporting; and
- sale-process support at exit.

## 3. Standard of Performance

RYDA will perform services in a commercially reasonable manner consistent with
the asset class, market, and approved operating budget. RYDA may use qualified
third-party vendors, dealers, mechanics, storage providers, captains, marinas,
transport providers, and insurance brokers.

## 4. Member Decisions Reserved

RYDA may not unilaterally approve:

- sale or replacement of the asset;
- uninsured modifications;
- material changes to the operating budget;
- capital calls;
- admission of new members; or
- dissolution of the LLC.

Those decisions remain subject to the Operating Agreement.

## 5. Fees and Reimbursements

The LLC pays RYDA the management fee listed in the final fee schedule. The LLC
also reimburses approved third-party operating costs, including insurance,
storage, detailing, maintenance, inspections, registration, and taxes.

RYDA may not mark up pass-through vendor invoices unless the final agreement
expressly permits it.

## 6. Records and Reporting

RYDA maintains an operating record for the LLC, including:

- asset mileage or engine-hour snapshots;
- service history;
- insurance status;
- damage and repair history;
- booking history;
- reserve balance reporting; and
- material vendor invoices.

Members receive quarterly condition and operating summaries.

## 7. Insurance and Risk

RYDA coordinates coverage, renewals, certificates, and claims support. Coverage
terms are controlled by the insurance policy, not this sample.

Members remain responsible for complying with operator eligibility rules,
approved-use rules, and claims-cooperation requirements.

## 8. Term and Termination

The agreement begins when the LLC acquires the asset and continues until the
asset is sold, the LLC is dissolved, or the members replace RYDA under the
Operating Agreement.

Termination for cause requires notice and an opportunity to cure, except for
fraud, willful misconduct, or other emergency conditions defined by counsel.

## 9. Transition Assistance

If RYDA is replaced, RYDA will provide commercially reasonable transition
support, including transfer of records, vendor contacts, insurance summaries,
and booking-calendar status.
