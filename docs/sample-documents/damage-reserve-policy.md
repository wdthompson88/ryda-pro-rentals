# RYDA Sample Damage Reserve Policy

Document status: redacted sample draft for review only.
Version: sample v0.1
Last updated: 2026-05-19

This sample explains how an LLC damage reserve may be used. Actual reserve
amounts and allocation rules are set by the Operating Agreement and insurance
policy.

## 1. Purpose

The damage reserve helps the LLC respond quickly to minor damage, deductibles,
inspection costs, and urgent repairs without waiting for a member capital call.

## 2. Funding

The reserve is funded at closing and replenished as needed through approved
operating budgets or member-approved capital calls.

## 3. Covered Uses

Reserve funds may be used for:

- insurance deductibles;
- minor cosmetic repairs;
- diagnostic inspections;
- towing or transport after an incident;
- vendor deposits;
- emergency repairs required to protect the asset; and
- claims-administration costs.

## 4. Attribution

Damage is attributed using handover photos, return photos, telematics where
available, service-provider observations, member statements, and insurance
claim findings.

## 5. Member Responsibility

If damage is caused by negligent, unauthorized, or prohibited use, the
responsible member may owe reimbursement beyond their pro-rata reserve
contribution, subject to the final Operating Agreement.

## 6. Insurance Claims

RYDA coordinates claim submission and repair logistics, but the carrier and
policy terms control coverage. Members must cooperate with claim investigation
and provide requested information promptly.

## 7. Reserve Reconciliation

Reserve activity is reported to members. Any remaining reserve at asset sale or
LLC dissolution is handled through the final distribution waterfall.
