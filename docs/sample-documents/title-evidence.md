# RYDA Sample Title Evidence

Document status: redacted sample.
Version: sample v0.1
Last updated: 2026-05-19

This sample shows the ownership and lien documentation RYDA expects before a
single-purpose LLC closes on an asset.

## 1. Asset

- Asset: `[YEAR MAKE MODEL]`
- VIN/HIN: `[REDACTED]`
- Seller: `[REDACTED]`
- Buyer: `[RYDA ASSET LLC]`
- Closing date: `[DATE]`

## 2. Evidence Reviewed

- current title or manufacturer statement of origin;
- bill of sale;
- lien release or payoff letter, if applicable;
- seller authority evidence;
- registration or documentation record;
- odometer statement, if required;
- tax and fee confirmation; and
- insurance binder.

## 3. Title Status

Sample status: clear title, pending final DMV or agency record update.

Any lien must be satisfied at closing or reflected in a counsel-approved
escrow/payoff process.

## 4. Exceptions

Known title exceptions, branded-title history, salvage history, flood history,
odometer discrepancy, or undisclosed liens must be disclosed to members before
closing.

## 5. Closing Checklist

- Confirm legal buyer name matches LLC formation record.
- Confirm VIN/HIN matches inspection report and insurance binder.
- Confirm seller wiring instructions through a verified channel.
- Confirm recorded title update is uploaded to the LLC document record.
