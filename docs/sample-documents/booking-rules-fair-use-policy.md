# RYDA Sample Booking Rules & Fair-Use Policy

Document status: redacted sample draft for review only.
Version: sample v0.1
Last updated: 2026-05-19

This policy explains how shared use is allocated among members. Final rules may
vary by asset, market, and insurance requirements.

## 1. Booking Entitlement

Each member receives booking access based on share count. Entitlement is shown
in the member portal and administered through the shared calendar.

## 2. Peak Windows

Peak windows are allocated so one member cannot monopolize the most desirable
dates. RYDA may limit repeat peak bookings until each member has had a fair
opportunity to reserve.

## 3. Advance Bookings

Members may request planned bookings in advance through the portal. Conflicts
are resolved using share count, prior usage, peak-window history, and the final
Operating Agreement rules.

## 4. Short-Notice Bookings

Short-notice bookings may be available when the asset is unreserved and service
status permits use. Short-notice bookings do not override confirmed planned
bookings.

## 5. Cancellations and No-Shows

Late cancellation, repeated cancellation, and no-show treatment is governed by
the final policy. RYDA may apply calendar restrictions or reserve charges when
member behavior harms other members' access.

## 6. Handover and Return

Members must complete handover and return steps, including photo documentation,
fuel or charge status, mileage or hour recording, and damage disclosure.

## 7. Guests and Additional Drivers

Only approved drivers or operators may control the asset. Guest access must
comply with insurance requirements and the Operating Agreement.

## 8. Prohibited Uses

Prohibited uses include racing, track use unless expressly approved, ride-share
or unauthorized commercial use, intoxicated operation, lending to unapproved
operators, and operation outside approved territory.
